## Frozen processing and estimand

1. Use the original, unchanged 18-gene IS and 13-gene SS panels and `log1p(count / total_UMI * 10,000)` expression values. Within each section z-standardize each gene with `ddof=1`; average available z-scores within each fixed panel. Call IS-high and SS-high at their section-specific 75th percentiles (`>=`). Record genes with nonzero within-section variance, score SDs, and realized high fractions. No gene, weight, threshold or patient subgroup is selected from observed effects.
2. Construct six nearest neighbors for **every filtered/in-tissue spot** in full-resolution pixel coordinates and keep directed edges no longer than 1.6 times that section's median nearest-neighbor pitch. Only after building the graph, retain edges whose source and target are each strictly `tumor` or `stroma`; all `other` and mixed tumor–stroma labels are excluded from the primary contrast. The exhaustive effect-blind external mapping is the previously locked crosswalk v1. The same broad classes are used for GSE294385, replacing its earlier finer Layer3 matching.
3. Restrict source spots to SS-low. For the fixed 96 Visium-lattice translations (16 at each hex radius 5–10, seed 42), map the intact target SS field to existing spots in the **same strict target pathology class**. Require at least 100 local strict IS-high edges before translation; at least 30 strict SS-low source edges in each IS group; at least 100 translation-compatible IS-high edges; and at least 10% translation-compatible coverage of local strict IS-high edges. Within each directed source-class/target-class pair, retain a comparison only if it has at least 30 SS-low-source edges in each IS group. For each accepted translation, calculate the min-group-count-weighted, pair-matched `(observed IS-high minus IS-low neighbor SS-high fraction) minus (shifted IS-high minus IS-low neighbor SS-high fraction)` on the **same compatible edges**. Require at least 24 accepted translations; the section estimate is their arithmetic mean. Record all 96 translation statuses. Spatial shifts are structured negative controls, not exchangeable permutations or 96 replicates.
4. Average valid section estimates within a patient; then take the equal-weighted mean across patients in each cohort. Report all section/patient estimates and failures, positive-patient count, the estimate in percentage points, a 10,000-draw patient-bootstrap percentile 95% interval (seed 42), and leave-one-patient-out range. No spot-level P value, translation-level P value, or cross-cohort pooled P value is planned. With at most six external patients, the bootstrap interval is descriptive and may be unstable.
5. The external six-patient result is the independent pathology-annotated assessment. Recomputed GSE294385 all-site and primary-only results are separately labeled context. Its previously reported Layer3-matched +1.65 percentage-point result is a **different estimand**, not the numerical comparator. Directed tumor-to-stroma edges are a prespecified feasibility/secondary description; no secondary result can rescue a failed primary estimate.
